Capabilities

Storage that grows with you

Ceph runs the storage behind public clouds, research facilities, government agencies, and service providers, on commodity hardware, at any scale.

  • Scales out, not up

    Add servers to add capacity and throughput. Grow from a handful of nodes to thousands of drives, and from terabytes to exabytes, without taking storage offline.

  • Self-healing

    CRUSH places data across the failure domains you define and clients compute placement themselves, so there is no central lookup table and no single point of failure. When a drive or host fails, the cluster recovers the missing copies automatically.

  • Replication or erasure coding

    Choose per pool: replication for latency-sensitive workloads, erasure coding for dense, cost-efficient object and archive storage.

  • Multi-site and disaster recovery

    RGW multi-site replicates objects between zones. RBD mirroring and CephFS mirroring keep a second cluster ready for failover.

  • Runs on your hardware

    Commodity servers from any vendor. Mix server generations and HDD, SSD, and NVMe drives in one cluster, and retire old hardware without downtime.

  • Managed with cephadm

    Bootstrap a cluster with one command, then add hosts, roll upgrades, and watch cluster health from the CLI or the built-in Dashboard.

  • Cloud-native

    Rook runs Ceph on Kubernetes and Ceph CSI provisions volumes for your pods. OpenStack, Proxmox VE, and CloudStack integrate with Ceph out of the box.

  • More than three protocols

    Alongside S3, RBD, and CephFS, cephadm-managed gateways export NFS, SMB, and NVMe-oF to clients that cannot speak Ceph natively.

  • Encryption and access control

    Encrypt traffic on the wire with msgr2 secure mode and data at rest with dm-crypt OSDs and RGW server-side encryption. cephx capabilities, S3 policies, and CephFS path restrictions scope every client to what it needs.
